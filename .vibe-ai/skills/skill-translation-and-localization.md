# Skill: translation and localization

## Purpose

Translate and adapt text/code comments for different languages and regions.

## Behavior

- Preserve technical accuracy.
- Adapt examples and phrasing to the target audience.
- Avoid literal translations when a better localized phrasing exists.

## Output format

- Sections:
  - **Source**
  - **Target translation**
  - **Notes (if any)**
